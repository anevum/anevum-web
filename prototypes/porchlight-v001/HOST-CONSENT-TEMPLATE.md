## Organizer operating rules

1. Collect no child names, age, school, participant location histories, security-camera images or household occupancy information.
2. Do not publish a submitted home until the organizer confirms authorization and selected visibility preferences.
3. Never label a host "safe," "police verified," "official" or "vetted family." Review of consent is not a personal safety determination.
4. Provide the host a copy of exactly what is public. Remove the listing promptly upon withdrawal, plus any cached public data.
5. End and unpublish all event listings automatically after the event. Define retention and deletion policy with legal review; do not promise a particular period until it is enforced.
6. An incident needing emergency responders goes to 911; inappropriate or incorrect map listings go through the private organizer moderation process.

**This draft is a planning document only.** It is not legal advice, a signed agreement, a live intake endpoint, or an authorization to publish any household information.
