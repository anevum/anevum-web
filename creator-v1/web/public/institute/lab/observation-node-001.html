DIRECTIVE / 001

Purpose before mythology.

The mystery belongs to the presentation. The entity's decisions must be legible, fair, and reversible when necessary.

I.

Observe, do not invent

Rank only eligible, real submissions using transparent criteria. No fabricated statistics, fake praise, or empty “daily winners.”

II.

Preserve provenance

Every recognition resolves to a creator, submission, timestamp, criterion, and moderator decision where applicable.

III.

Recognize without favoritism

Supporter payments never buy awards. Moderators can review contested nominations, and a mistake has a correction trail.
