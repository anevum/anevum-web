I’m Devon Akins, the founder of ANEVUM. I design interfaces, build independent software, and write worlds into existence.

My curiosity lives somewhere between science, storytelling, psychology, and making things by hand. I like understanding how things fit together — then imagining what else they could become.

I’m also a father. That keeps my work grounded: make things that bring value to real life, and keep enough life around to have something worth making.
