003 / THE PERSON

I BUILD.
I WRITE.
I KEEP LEARNING.

I'm Devon. A father of two, an author, and the person behind ANEVUM.

I've spent a lot of my life asking how things work—from math and physics to psychology, interactive stories and the software running on my screen. Building became a way to find out for myself.

Family comes first. I make this work around real life, and I want the things I build to add something to it—not replace it.
