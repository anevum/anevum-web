Useful comes first.

If someone cannot understand or use a product, the polish does not save it. Good design makes the next action clear and worth taking.

02 / UNDERSTAND THE WHOLE

Nothing exists in isolation.

I like figuring out how the whole thing works, from the interface to the logic behind it. That is where my curiosity about math and science shows up.

03 / KEEP THE WONDER

Leave room to play.

Games, stories and strange ideas belong here too. I want to make practical things without designing all the fun out of them.

04 / THE PERSON DOING THE WORK

A dad.
A writer.
A builder.

I’m Devon Akins. I’m a father of two, an author, and the person building ANEVUM. I design interfaces and create independent software, games and stories.

I’m drawn to science, mathematics, psychology, writing, and making things by hand. I follow questions into projects, sometimes much further than expected. That curiosity is a strength, but finishing and shipping the work matters more than collecting ideas.

Family comes first. I build around that life rather than pretending work is the only thing I am. The projects here are independent, evolving, and honestly presented.
