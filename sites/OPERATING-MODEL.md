## Core positioning

**A local maker delivering fast, high-quality, fixed-scope websites at prices that small businesses can test without an agency budget.** Compete on efficiency and simplicity. The studio uses ANEVUM's midnight/navy Continuance Institute identity; **client websites should reflect each client's brand, not the studio's cosmic appearance**. Quality matters more than animation count. Respect reduced-motion settings, good typography, accessibility and page speed.

## Lead-to-delivery workflow

1. **Research (15 minutes max).** Identify small businesses with outdated or absent sites. Verify the business exists and confirm current public details from official/contact sources; use placeholders for anything uncertain.
2. **Uncommissioned concept (20–30 minutes max).** Create a distinctive *partial* concept only: compelling hero, layout, contact/visit flow. Add clearly visible "not the official website" disclosure, no use of copyrighted logos/photos without rights, no fake owner quotes, reviews, affiliations or unlicensed stock media. No ecommerce for regulated goods.
3. **In-person pitch (under five minutes).** Show preview quickly on phone. Give a QR code to the studio or preview. Ask whether a fixed-scope $49/$99/$199 package would solve an immediate issue. Never say the site is already theirs.
4. **Scope + permission.** Ask for business-controlled domain, approved brand/name, text, legally usable images, exact pages/sections, one point of contact and deadline. Agree price, revisions and maintenance terms in writing. Confirm the client consents to public publication.
5. **Build + verify.** Start from tested layout modules; customize typography, spacing, imagery and content. Check on 320/390/768/1440 widths, test telephone/map links, headings, keyboard, reduced motion, privacy/security, and favicon/social preview where included.
6. **Approval, payment, transfer.** Obtain written signoff. Use a reputable payment provider or appropriate bank transfer; do not collect card details directly in site forms. Collect the agreed amount before transferring deliverables and publishing on client-owned domain. Provide owner-accessible files and DNS/hosting handoff instructions.
7. **Follow-up.** Offer separately contracted support, request permission to feature final work or testimonial, archive unsuccessful pitches, record time spent, track next agreed client action.

Do not solicit permission to use private business information by scraping; do not publish passwords, partner/client details, notes, account credentials or unverified contact info.

## Standardized deliverables

Base deliverable: responsive web files, text/photo assets the owner authorized, title/meta basics, accessible section structure, live links tested, handoff instructions. Client controls its domain account and receives the agreed copyright and content permissions after payment, excluding third-party licenses.

**Two client review rounds mean two consolidated batches of requested changes.** New pages, major change in scope, product upload databases, booking calendars, shopping carts, payment flow, voice/video, user accounts, automatic social posting or legal text beyond templates trigger a new quote.

No promised search ranking, traffic, sales conversion, payment processing, or zero fees. Cloudflare free tiers and service prices can change; the customer should have a clear plan if quotas or domains cost money.

## Minimal systems that exist and what remains

Built: landing page, curated retail demo, dynamic generic preview generator, owner-only pipeline and field editor, existing D1 records/revisions, basic prelaunch health route.

Needed before commercial intake: independent browser/mobile checks, one signed-in owner CRUD test, a short client scope agreement and invoice template, proof-of-domain-ownership flow, public policy/privacy/contact review, a delivery checklist, backup/rollback, and business/legal/payment setup as applicable.

Future after first 3 paid clients: reusable archetypes for restaurant/professional service/retail/creative/events, image uploads with licensed asset tracking, template cloning, quote builder, reminders, automated screenshot QA, approval links, invoicing provider integration, separately hosted client domains with isolation and transfer.

**No website editing platform or hosting SaaS is promised in this pilot.** Avoid maintaining dozens of bespoke sites under a single opaque account without a viable handoff plan.
