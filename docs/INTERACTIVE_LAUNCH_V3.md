# ANEVUM Interactive Launch V3

Date: 2026-09-15

## Product shape

ANEVUM.com remains REPLY-first for the first-book launch. Public visitors should understand the book within seconds. RHENLINK is the persistent identity layer that can be entered from the public launch without forcing visitors through the future platform architecture.

## Interaction language

- The reading and book-selling surfaces stay literary, cinematic, restrained, and human.
- The navigation layer may behave like a terminal: animated command drawer, typed system lines, keyboard access, motion, and identity state.
- The terminal treatment must remain navigation/interface chrome rather than turning REPLY itself into a HUD or game dashboard.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## RHENLINK launch contract

- Email/password sign-up and sign-in remain backed by the existing ANEVUM member Supabase project.
- Public member metadata may include display name, handle, bio, status line, equipped title, avatar treatment, Circle preference, and visibility preferences.
- User-editable metadata is presentation data only. It is never authorization.
- Admin/moderator/privileged access stays in protected app metadata or server-owned records.
- Profile edits must persist across refresh and sign-out/sign-in.

## Progression presentation

- Level is a long-term participation measure, not authority, skill rating, popularity, moral worth, or paid status.
- Everyday display uses a compact Rank Mark; the full profile can use a larger animated Level Artifact.
- XP should always show progress to the next level, not only a raw total.
- Achievements are individual badges/honors with earned/locked states, descriptive copy, XP value, and milestone time when available.

## Public launch navigation

Primary public destinations for Generation 1:

1. REPLY
2. The Story
3. The Book / Buy
4. RHENLINK

Future Wiki, LATTICE, Commons, Worlds, Store expansion, and other modules remain latent until they have launch-worthy content and functionality.
