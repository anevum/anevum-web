## Art principles

1. **Objects, not badges.** Every artifact must read as a singular manufactured/discovered object with a useful silhouette and believable thickness; the old 256px SVGs are fallback UI sketches, not the hero aesthetic.

2. **Material truth.** Favor engineered ivory, mineral ceramic, aged metal, blackglass and polished crystal with consistent reflectance, facets, joints, wear and refraction. Every cut, joint and suspension should be physically coherent even if the object is speculative.

3. **Composed illumination.** One primary source and one supporting reflected light; dense darkness creates room for precious illumination. Prevent excessive neon, uniformly glowing outlines and indiscriminate bloom.

4. **Continuance restraint.** Instrumental, archival and quietly advanced—never steampunk collage, occult symbology, fantasy inventory kits, generic HUD typography, pseudo-scientific diagrams or random decorative stars.

5. **Identity through change.** Each 10-level chapter widens construction, dimension and conceptual ambition without arbitrarily increasing brightness or implying paid rarity. Every item stays distinct from adjacent levels.

6. **One subject, many scales.** A native master dictates hero/collection variants; a hand-simplified silhouette or existing SVG supports 16–32px micro use. A thumbnail is not a second invented artifact.

7. **Recognition is earned, not staged.** Use clearly identified preview states, lock overlays and accessible status text. Cosmetic artwork cannot grant XP, permissions, votes or financial benefits.

## Palette and material propositions (not final brand tokens)

- **Void / absolute shadow:** #0C111C; supporting mineral midnight #1B2230. Avoid featureless crushed black.

- **Mineral ivory:** #EAE8DC; use for structure or pinlight, not a blanket background.

- **Aged copper:** #AA744F; subtle oxidation, shallow etched metal and honest edges.

- **Soft auric metal:** #D6B781; warm pearlescent gold; no trophy-yellow gradients.

- **Night sapphire:** #365E9A; bright glint #83B3EC, with deep-blue glass underneath.

- **Blackglass violet:** #625B8F; optional restrained interference blue/violet, not a global UI neon.

- **Ember core:** #EAA06E; used as a selective material property, not a universal XP color.

- **Palette rule:** one dominant dark or mineral material, one metal, at most one primary emissive color per artifact. Keep contrast independent of hue and test color-blind states. These hexes are *art-direction experiments* and must not override approved ANEVUM site typography and theme tokens.

## Optics, camera and master composition

- Studio-quality isolated 3/4 object view, stable 70–100mm-equivalent perspective, realistic highlights and thickness; center of mass stays inside a generous transparent-safe zone.

- Master art: propose a **native, non-upscaled 2048×2048 minimum**, preferred 3072×3072 lossless transparent PNG (or editable layered original). Existing 1254×1254 original PNGs are the high-detail reference/fidelity baseline only, not automatically signed-off final masters.

- Single soft key at upper left, restrained cool opposing rim, plausible ambient fill. A dark/bright checkerboard alpha test must find no fringing or accidental black matting; do not bake drop shadows into transparent edges.

- Transparent object occupies ~72–84% of the square master; leave breathing room for floating/suspended parts. Use an identical notional camera scale within each arc, except an explicitly documented extreme silhouette.

- No numbers, titles, UI labels, forged texture labels or unreadable text baked into the image. Level number, artifact title, state and description remain accessible DOM text.

## Six visual families (assignment, not rank)

**Signals & Resonance:** emitters, receivers, beacons, tuned elements. **Keys & Orientation:** access mechanisms, sights, navigation. **Living & Botanical Relics:** organized growth, moving forms and hybrid biotic craft. **Engines & Mechanisms:** torque, timing, energy, conversion. **Seals & Vessels:** objects of identity, protection, containment. **Cosmological Structures:** scale, spheres, thresholds and durable construction.

Families share production grammar, not identical frames. A common gold circle or ornate shield on everything is specifically rejected.

## Six editorial arcs (no independent XP tiers)

- **01–10 / Ignition:** Invitation / small sparks and recognizably useful instruments.

- **11–20 / Calibration:** Tuning, listening, registering and shaping.

- **21–30 / Orientation:** Deliberate measurements, bearings and first maps.

- **31–40 / Correspondence:** Two systems learning to affect one another.

- **41–50 / Construction:** Complex instruments, structures, thresholds and trust.

- **51–60 / Continuance:** Balanced mechanisms, expanded scale and shared light.

## Motion and accessible interaction

- Level-up: ANEVUM mark pulse -> measured expansion -> artifact appears with directional energy moving *inside the object* -> readable level/title/receipt -> graceful retraction. Animation remains subordinate to message clarity and does not fabricate earned state.

- Notification preference and reduced-motion: no forced flashing or camera shake; short crossfade or static composed art with a persistent history entry. Pause/restore logic and status announcements follow V031, not bespoke artwork code.

- Dark and light surface checks, high-contrast mode, screen readers, 200% zoom, keyboard navigation, 320px mobile. When adjacent text names an artifact, image is decorative unless it adds meaningful independent information; otherwise concise factual alt text.

- Preview controls must say **Preview / Not earned / No XP**. Locked pieces cannot glow as if awarded, and client UI never determines unlock eligibility.

## Review and acceptance scorecard

Each of twelve prototype masters must be reviewed at 1024px, 512px, 96px, 32px and 16px, plus on a real iPhone. Score 1–5 for (1) uniqueness/readability, (2) believable material physics, (3) Continuance/ANEVUM restraint, (4) lighting/depth and (5) miniature clarity. **Proposal:** mean >=4.2 across categories and no category below 3.5. Numeric scores organize review but never substitute for explicit owner approval.

Hard rejections: obvious generated-edge or transparency artifacts; repeated silhouette with different gem color; incorrect artifact name/level; text baked into image; impossible joints without deliberate conceptual rationale; low-res blur, oversaturation, unrelated canon claims or unreadable locked status.

Milestone gates: (A) confirm doctrine/twelve anchor briefs -> (B) create 3 direction variations of levels 1,30,60 and select one visual grammar -> (C) produce/approve remaining 9 anchor masters -> (D) produce remaining 48 masters under recorded source version -> (E) create responsive derivatives + accessibility + art manifest -> (F) test staging visuals and owner device sign-off -> (G) separately authorize any public release.
