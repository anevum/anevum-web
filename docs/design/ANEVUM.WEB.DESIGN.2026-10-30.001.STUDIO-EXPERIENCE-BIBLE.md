## 0. The one-sentence product

ANEVUM is an independent, family-rooted creative studio founded by Devon Akins. It makes original fictional universes, games, stories, and freely modifiable creative software.

## 1. Decisions to preserve

- ANEVUM is the studio and public development identity. Devon Akins is the founder and creator; devonakins.com is the personal and author portfolio.
- TRANSCOSMIC is the original fictional universe; the Continuance Institute is a fictional institution inside it, NOT the real-world legal company or studio identity.
- SEVERANCE is the first major TRANSCOSMIC game and currently a tested technical prototype, not a public multiplayer game. Do not represent the persistent PvPvE world as implemented.
- ABSENCE PROTOCOL is a preserved horror concept. Family adventure work is experimental. No unapproved personal projects become commercial studio announcements.
- RHEN and trading are separate technical work, not the public ANEVUM mission.
- Cristy's healthcare apps, IdeaFlow and CristyBreathe are separate business/work products with clearly documented rights, data protection, and branding.
- Brandle and Brandicade are personal gifts and independent experiments. Public source or Worker availability does not imply permission to promote them as official ANEVUM games. Do not automatically place them in the studio game catalogue or news.
- All ANEVUM software remains free, self-hostable and genuinely open source. The creative canon, expressive artwork, and trademarks require separate rights statements, not a blanket software license.
- Keep the approved Orbital Underground logo and existing functioning code, routes, accounts, member data, Studio systems, and authorized creator publishing. Do not rebuild the backend or expose private data.
- Maintain a sustainable scope that complements family, writing, publishing, and professional work rather than competing with those priorities.

## 3. Primary audiences and jobs

Visitor who found a game: recognize the studio, find the game and its genuine play status within one interaction.
Reader/world fan: enter TRANSCOSMIC, understand the premise, find approved lore and real stories.
Independent developer: see what is actually shipped, inspect source and setup details.
Potential collaborator: identify who built the work, understand licensing, contact the real founder through existing verified channels.
Returning follower: find recent meaningful development with source-linked evidence.
Existing member: reach protected account, Creator Studio and community tools without a forced new onboarding experience.

The public experience should never require an account to read about a world, game, or the studio.

## 4. Public navigation and route ownership

- Home / — studio entry, environment, featured game, latest verified progress and concise links.
- Worlds /worlds — TRANSCOSMIC, approved world overview and links to published stories/game contexts.
- Games /games — first major game and intentionally announced independent experiences; status of each project is explicit.
- Journal /journal — dated, source-backed development dispatches and release records.
- Studio /about — founder, practice, values, software source and licensing distinctions.

Existing /severance is the canonical game dossier. Do not create a competing /games/severance route unless carefully redirected.
Existing /discover, /community, /creators, /status and /guide remain reachable via appropriate secondary navigation.
Existing /studio is a PROTECTED creator workstation and must never be reused for public About/Studio.
Existing /member, /library and staff routes retain authorization and information boundaries.
Unknown or retired routes must not silently become public marketing pages.

Avoid simultaneous duplicate public navigation systems. On public studio pages, the visible header and its drawer are sufficient; preserve the V136 console on legacy, member and editor routes. IREN remains available through deliberate control, not a blocking floating widget on reading pages. Do not disable its expanded, accessibility, or protected assistance functionality.

## 5. Home storyboard

Scene 0 / First paint: real Orbital Underground emblem, readable headline, stable accessible navigation and immediate buttons before cinematic extras load.
Scene 1 / Entrance: original dimensional orbital architecture behind 'Original worlds. Built to be explored.' Include founder attribution and two clear destinations: TRANSCOSMIC and Games. Motion should feel slow and spatial, not like a screensaver.
Scene 2 / Creative practice: Worlds / Games / Technology. Give each a distinct silhouette and function; no fabricated live counters or empty promises. Use original vectors or original first-party materials.
Scene 3 / Feature: SEVERANCE, with original approved illustration or cinematic media. Status visibly says prototype in development and no public multiplayer access. Distinguish concept media from actual gameplay.
Scene 4 / Evidence: one strong real development update followed by an understated journal link. Subsequent chronological records live on /journal, not a fake activity ticker.
Scene 5 / Public creator archive: OPTIONAL and clearly separated from the first-party studio catalogue. Render only if genuine approved records exist.
Scene 6 / Studio/founder: real name, independent status, values, GitHub and personal site. End with one meaningful CTA.

A page can be cinematic and still load incrementally. No full-screen preloader that blocks navigation or a mandatory interactive 3D introduction.

## 6. Inner pages

WORLD /worlds:
- Clear distinction between the ANEVUM studio and TRANSCOSMIC fiction.
- Entrance uses a distant gateway, orbital networks, suggestive scale and mysterious unexplored spaces.
- Present approved continuity, fictional geographies, original story fragments and the SEVERANCE connection.
- Canon is locked only by explicit review. Draft names, creatures, biotechnological ideas, locations and untested world details must be marked provisional or withheld.
- A future interactive atlas is allowed only when real navigable content exists; never build an empty map for decorative complexity.

GAMES /games:
- SEVERANCE is the main focus. Show premise, medium, status, development footage when available and direct game dossier.
- Small independent games appear only after intentional public release and ownership checks.
- Concepts such as ABSENCE PROTOCOL remain clearly marked concept rather than playable.
- Do not automatically advertise personal gifts, family names or child-related material. Public code does not make a private relationship a studio product.
- Avoid 'Play now' buttons unless the link reaches an actual verified working build.

SEVERANCE /severance:
- Mature horror treatment: dimmed containment infrastructure, environmental fracture, unsettling biological/technological traces, silence and rare warning illumination.
- The dramatic story is separate from the factual developer status. Prose can be evocative; badges, CTA and footage cannot imply launched systems.
- Story premise: a world cut off from the larger network, with surviving communities living inside its isolation. The cause, game rules, and named geography remain canon-controlled.
- Sequence: cinematic incident / world premise / player activity / verified playable prototype / development roadmap / source + updates.
- First playable target: one shelter-to-facility return loop, a clear objective, evidence recovery and one meaningful stalker; validate this before expanding into persistent online territory.
- Fullscreen and controllers may become production requirements but are not represented as already implemented for the public game.

JOURNAL /journal:
- Editorial logs, not simulated transmissions. Each entry: exact date, category, title, one useful change, link to verifiable commit/PR/release, status where needed.
- Events are chronologically sorted. Private clinical, financial, access, or family information never appears.
- Technical details can be expanded, but the visible summary explains what the change enables for people.
- A source merge alone is not proof of live deployment.

STUDIO /about:
- Founder Devon Akins, independent work, family-rooted continuity and honest development.
- Three pillars: Originality; Continuity; Craft with evidence.
- Clear distinction between permissive software licenses, artwork/canon permissions and studio trademarks.
- Links to personal writing and GitHub, without manufacturing credentials, staff counts or an agency offering.
- Avoid negative, defensive marketing sentences about what ANEVUM is not. Use the boundaries internally and publish affirmative descriptions.

## 7. Approved visual language

Art direction: 'Orbital Underground meets cinematic archival science fiction.'
Studio atmosphere: obsidian, machined graphite, warm bone, dim copper, restricted violet and icy signal light.
TRANSCOSMIC: immense scale, glacial light, transit architecture, unknown horizons, patient motion.
SEVERANCE: containment decay, diseased technology, selectively lit crimson and severe environmental tension.
Personal/family games: unique bright or playful styles as appropriate; never apply horror styling automatically.
The Continuance Institute's visual authority belongs to fictional media, not real legal/studio copy.

Retain the first-party Orbital Underground symbol geometry. Header logo, favicon and social art must use the same silhouette. The 03 Orbital Underground mark is not a generic atom or a new triangular logo.

Color authority and fallback tokens:
- Void: #09080E; deep panel: #17131F; raised metal: #221B2A
- Warm bone: #EFE6DA; secondary text: #C8BDBA
- Copper: #D2A57F; violet: #B49BCE; cold light: #A8CBD2
- SEVERANCE-only warning accent: muted blood red, not a generic studio CTA
- Focus, warning, success and error states have accessibility-driven contrast, not decorative meaning.

Typography:
- Editorial headings: Iowan Old Style / Baskerville / Palatino / Georgia fallback; large display sizes vary by context.
- Functional interface: Inter / Segoe UI / system-ui; readable 15-18px body, restrained small caps.
- Instrument labels and timelines: IBM Plex Mono / Consolas / monospace; only short labels, not long paragraphs.
- Special game wordmarks may use a condensed industrial display face if legally available; no tiny illegible decoration.
- The font system must work without external network fonts.

Composition:
- Max readable paragraph width ~66 characters.
- Strong asymmetrical hero with visual object on one side and message on the other; collapse gracefully on phones.
- Different section compositions, not endless equal-sized dark cards.
- Generous whitespace, consistent vertical rhythm and clear focus.
- Foreground hero copy and critical navigation cannot depend on a video, WebGL context or network image loading.

## 8. Motion / sound / 3D

- Orbital logo ambient cycle: approximately 35-50 seconds, low amplitude, never required for comprehension.
- Studio atmosphere: layered parallax and faint light shifts, GPU-friendly transform/opacity; no constant repaints of giant blurred layers.
- Interaction response: 140-240ms; drawer transitions ~280-420ms; editorial reveals ~350-600ms only as progressive enhancement.
- No mandatory scroll hijacking, aggressive cursor-chasing, or random glitch on every click.
- Reserve blood, alarming distortion and heavier visual effects for SEVERANCE contexts; expose a still poster on reduced motion.
- Sound off by default. Any sound, haptics or controller feedback is intentional and disabled when unsupported or unwanted.
- The actual game may have immersive cinematic video; do not relabel storyboard concept art as gameplay.
- Prefer first-party video/screen capture, approved original illustration, SVG, and reproducible 3D assets. No stock-feeling AI-generated filler.
- WebGL must degrade cleanly to a polished static/dimensional background; pause expensive effects when tab is hidden.

## 9. Navigation behavior

Desktop >=1280: compact studio header with emblem, Home/Worlds/Games/Journal/Studio, search, account and a drawer for secondary routes. A tiny contextual progress indicator is optional; it must not duplicate global navigation.
Tablet: preserve readable labels where possible, then reduce to emblem, search, account and menu.
Phone 320-600: no overlapping fixed bottom console on the five studio editorial pages or SEVERANCE dossier; top header and accessible menu own navigation.
IREN assistance is invoked from header/drawer when needed; an idle companion should not float above active text on public editorial pages.
Protected Creator Studio and member pages retain their working V136 controls until a separately reviewed, permission-safe redesign.
All buttons have visible keyboard focus, 44px-ish mobile targets, clear names, and actionable destinations.
No decorative controls that do nothing.

## 10. Status language and truth

Canonical project states:
- IDEA — discussion only, no technical prototype
- CONCEPT — approved creative direction or design artifacts
- PROTOTYPE — tested partial implementation; public access not implied
- PLAYABLE PREVIEW — verified working accessible build and limitations
- RELEASED — verified live version, support and documented release record
- ARCHIVED — intentionally retained, no active roadmap promise

A project can use 'pre-alpha' as supporting detail, not as proof that a public game is available.
Unverified numbers, fictional engagement metrics, simulated notifications, incomplete checkout, misleading member unlocks, and fake screenshots are prohibited.
Each game page must state what is available to try TODAY and link to the source record when feasible.

## 11. Media and performance

Studio hero media: original 4K master can exist in asset production, but browsers receive adaptive, compressed, responsive derivatives and poster assets.
Prefer short seamless loop with real first-party 3D/cinematic composition over several heavy videos on one page.
Load text/logo and CTA first. Lazy-load all nonessential media and 3D.
Target real-user p75 Core Web Vitals: LCP <= 2.5s, CLS <= .10, INP <= 200ms where technically achievable; measure rather than claim performance.
Test at 1440, 1024, 768, 390 and 320 widths; slow network, reduced motion, keyboard-only navigation, iOS Safari, Android Chrome and modern desktop.
No broken asset paths, missing fallbacks, overlapping fixed controls, clipped display headings, inaccessible dialogs, false 'open now' claims, or delayed page content behind animation.

## 13. Creative and business alignment

ANEVUM is sustainable, not a replacement for finishing books or supporting family and Cristy's ventures.
Portfolio attribution lives on devonakins.com, with code repository links from ANEVUM where relevant.
TRANSCOSMIC material becomes source for fiction, game chapters, narrative art and perhaps future media; each derivative remains canon-reviewed.
Creator technology exists because the studio needs it, not because ANEVUM must become a subscription marketplace.
No new payment processor, proprietary tier, speculative economy or costly always-on server is introduced by the website redesign.
Measure success by completed creative artifacts, working playable experiences, honest documentation and maintenance cost rather than account or follower counts without genuine demand.

## 14. Acceptance and release gates

A. Narrative: a stranger can correctly explain ANEVUM, TRANSCOSMIC and SEVERANCE in 15-30 seconds without confusing the fictional institute for an actual company.
B. Architecture: every visible action leads somewhere real; studio navigation is singular; old protected routes remain available and authenticated as before.
C. Art: first-party emblem appears consistently; atmospheric media has clear approval/origin; game concept illustration is labelled.
D. Responsive: 320/390/768/1024/1440 checks, safe-area treatment, no dock/IREN obscuring editorial copy, no clipped headline.
E. Accessibility: keyboard, Escape and focus return for dialogs; reduced-motion and static media path; readable touch targets and contrast.
F. Truth: no fake launches, data, release counts or private content; journal links verify.
G. Technical: strict TypeScript, production build, current browser QA, protected route regressions, screenshot review and actual asset integrity.
H. Deployment: identify current live Worker/domain/route ownership, compare exact source SHA, verify preview with media present, rehearse rollback, promote only with controlled release and read-back.
I. Exit criterion: the site is an effective entrance to actual creative work, not an excuse to restart a social platform.
