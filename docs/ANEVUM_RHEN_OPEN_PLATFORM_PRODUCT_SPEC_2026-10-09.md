# ANEVUM.RHEN.PACKAGE.2026-10-09.002.OPEN-PLATFORM-OVERHAUL
## Product and member experience specification

**Prepared:** 2026-10-09. **Status:** APPROVED DIRECTION / PROPOSED IMPLEMENTATION; NOT RELEASED.
**Owners:** RHEN product; ANEVUM Web implements member account, billing and public/member experience.
**Authority:** A preparation package only. No production payment, brokerage, strategy, data-use or publication changes are authorized.

## 1. Purpose

RHEN is an independently built platform for learning markets, testing ideas with evidence, conducting reproducible research and, in separately approved future releases, operating personal automated trading tools. Its promise is **Learn the markets. Test your ideas. Understand the results.**

Accessibility and evidence are core. Do not market six-figure income, effortless wealth, guaranteed profitability, risk-free AI trading, a wealthy lifestyle, or an investment return from subscribing. Teach differences among wages, saving, long-term investing and short-term speculation. Rewards should not promote dangerous trading.

ANEVUM remains a broader personal workshop; RHEN is the first application. GRAEN, VELUM, IREN and NOSTRA remain RHEN internal responsibilities and are not new top-level paid apps.

## 2. Access model and migration

**RHEN Open: free.** Public research/evidence and education, personal Command, member RHEN workspace, saved strategy drafts, free capped simulations/paper experiments when capacity and backend permit, safe account export/delete, and research participation when moderated beta is approved. No credit card or compulsory conversion.

**RHEN Supporter: voluntary.** People who want to fund ANEVUM can display an optional, restrained profile/terminal badge or theme and receive thanks if they opt in. No trading advantage, research-rank boost, compute priority, profit-sharing, algorithm access or financial recommendation. Proposed test offers: $12 yearly; optional $1 monthly if economically appropriate; optional one-time $3, $5 and $10 support. No prices or Stripe products are approved by this package. Do not imply tax deductibility or charitable status.

**RHEN Compute: bounded incremental capacity.** Free fair-use allowance, then optional extra pre-disclosed quotas for hosted paper simulation and larger research jobs, only after actual user-level cost measurement. Not priced per trade, percentage of returns, deposits, buying power or number of profitable signals. First pilot remains FREE with enforced caps. No surprise overages, auto-reloads, transferrable value, cash redemption or broker cash custody.

**RHEN Live: separate future service.** No price or live member execution has been approved. Paid/supporter status never grants broker permissions. Later release needs independent broker/OAuth approval, legal review, owner authorization, risk-state recovery tests and explicit member trading consent.

**Important:** Existing RHEN Cloud live-mode Stripe product and inactive $2.99/$4.99 prices are a superseded proposed commercial packaging. Do not activate, silently rebrand or delete these records. Keep the existing billing branch and its cancellation, customer-identity and webhook-safety work. A new supporter/compute catalog requires explicit new review. Pending sandbox tests in issue #241 remain useful and should be completed safely.

## 3. Who we serve

1. Curious adults who want a grounded introduction to markets without an expensive course or profit promises.
2. Self-directed traders who wish to test strategies scientifically, understand costs and risk, and learn whether a hypothesis survived independent validation.
3. Independent researchers and developers who can contribute reproducible critiques and improvements.
4. Members who choose to support the workshop and/or require additional compute; payments are not the core measure of participant value.

Never assume more users automatically means more revenue. Contributions, referrals, independent tests and opt-in findings can generate value, but user brokerage assets and profits remain theirs and do not become ANEVUM revenue merely through usage.

## 4. First-use journey

1. **Discover RHEN:** accurate feature availability; paper vs live vs simulation clearly marked. No mock data masquerading as results.
2. **Join for free:** verified member identity and own private Command; no card.
3. **Learn the essentials:** optional short learning path plus a required safety/risks acknowledgment before hosted paper execution. Learning itself remains available to everyone.
4. **Create an experiment:** choose an approved strategy family, paper starting capital, maximum exposure and loss/risk limits; distinguish local replay, RHEN internal paper simulation and separately linked Alpaca paper account.
5. **Start with explicit consent:** admitted paper job becomes durable; user sees version, ownership, market-data timestamp, job quota and source.
6. **Observe and reflect:** simulated decisions, entries/exits, skips, errors, time periods, costs/slippage assumptions, P&L and drawdown; comparisons always include baseline and uncertainty.
7. **Share optional research:** privately draft findings; explicit separate consent to submit sanitized methods/results to moderation. No private broker data is published by default.
8. **Support or expand optionally:** opt-in support page separate from measured-capacity checkout. Never prompt for payment because a strategy lost money.

## 5. Learning curriculum, first release

Publish a COMPLETE small set first; do not create empty promise pages for all content. Candidate modules:

1. **Financial objectives and reality:** income vs investment returns, required capital, compounding, risk and time/family opportunity cost.
2. **How equity markets work:** stock, ETF, sessions, liquidity, spread, account types, cash-account settlement and changing broker rules.
3. **Orders and fills:** market/limit, stop logic, slippage, partial fills, paper vs real, broker and after-hours risks.
4. **Risk arithmetic:** percent vs dollar P&L, drawdowns, trade expectancy, position sizing, maximum loss, why an attractive win rate may lose money.
5. **Turning an idea into rules:** hypotheses, entry/exit logic, data inputs, parameters vs rule-set changes.
6. **Validating ideas:** transaction cost, look-ahead and survivorship bias, development/validation/holdout and replay parity limits.
7. **Reading RHEN evidence:** live private account vs public-safe aggregate vs broker paper vs internal simulated vs historical hypothetical. Missing data stays marked missing.
8. **Responsible participation:** no guarantee of profit, when not to trade, risk boundaries, reporting errors, long-term investment alternatives.

Every published lesson must have accessible plain language, example calculations and sources, quiz answers with explanations, version/review date, source evidence, correction history, and text alternatives to charts. Educational completion is not a professional qualification or trading competence license.

## 6. Research community: structured evidence, not a copy of Reddit

**Do not launch an unmoderated forum.** Start with a curated evidence catalog and a closed submission pilot. Member research objects are QUESTION, HYPOTHESIS, EXPERIMENT, REPLICATION, REVIEW and CORRECTION; connect records by citations and revisions.

EXPERIMENT fields: title, summary, owner pseudonym, method, version/fingerprint, data source and period, allowed universe, sample size, cost/friction assumptions, validation/holdout separation, outcome and limits, citation list, content warnings, consent scope, moderation state and immutable history.

Lifecycle: private draft -> submitted -> needs revision -> independently reproduced / not yet verified -> archived / retracted. Reproduction claims require independent data and reviewer record; publishing is not an endorsement or strategy promotion.

Contributor standing comes from accurate citations, successful reproductions, useful reviews and corrections. **Never** from support payment, deposits, trade volume, public P&L, streaks, or raw popularity alone. Public identity is pseudonymous by default. Reputation must be transparent and appealable, not an invitation to speculation.

Require reporting, rate limits, review queues, anti-spam, edits/version history, citation/retention/license policies, safeguards against coordinated security promotion/market manipulation, and simple moderator controls. Individual security recommendations and buy/sell signals do not belong in open public research records. Public user-generated submissions are OFF until human moderation capacity is proved.

Member-owned raw fills, orders, equities, account identifiers and strategies are confidential. Improving the company algorithm from member experiments requires explicit opt-in, defined purpose and rights, separate from account signup and payment. Withdrawals affect future use; document lawful record retention and past published derivative limitations before enrollment.

## 7. Supporter experience

Possible routes: /me/support (account), /me/usage (capacity), /products/rhen/learn (public), /products/rhen/research (curated public), /apps/rhen/learn and /apps/rhen/research (authenticated), /apps/rhen/terminal (private), /me/billing (existing gated billing account). Final paths should preserve redirects and registry conventions.

Support copy: "Support ANEVUM's independent development" and "Optional contribution. No investment, trading benefit, guaranteed result or charitable tax deduction." Badge and themes are purely cosmetic. Default supporters are anonymous publicly. Account owner can hide recognition. Cancel means no surprise renewals; receipts and self-service management always available.

Supporter is NOT a moderator role or a brokerage entitlement. Support does not unlock extra positions or greater trading risk. Stripe customer and agreement records must be server-owned, isolated per member, and included in account export/deletion handling.

## 8. Fair resource allowances

Initial suggested structure (subject to measurement): 1 paper workspace/member; 1 admitted hosted paper bot/member if global capacity permits; limited bounded replay experiments/week; limited concurrent research jobs and data/history ranges. Free capacity is a real benefit, not a 7-day bait-and-switch. No unlimited compute or guaranteed execution slots.

Run shared market data and signals once where licensing permits; allocate bounded independent per-tenant execution. Dormant accounts should consume near-zero compute. Every user sees exact quota/remaining allowance/reset/source freshness. Quota exhausted -> stop accepting NEW experiments/entries with precise explanation; reconcile and finish safe exits/reductions. Never charge retroactively.

Pricing research starts only after measured average and P95 CPU/memory per active member, broker/API usage, licensing, durable storage, error/retry volume and support effort. Sell predictable bounded service capacity, not stored money or high-frequency "pay per signal" incentives.

## 9. Navigation and UX

Public RHEN hub shows actual current features, real source status, free onboarding and public-safe methodology. Member Command is a personal home across ANEVUM apps. Member RHEN shows own simulator/broker connection and experiment state, not the founder account. Learning and research are first-class sections, but don't displace the terminal. Use current restrained ANEVUM workshop visual design and real ANEVUM marks, not exaggerated trading aesthetics.

Terminal states: NOT-CONNECTED, DRAFT, READY-PAPER, RUNNING-PAPER, PAUSED, QUOTA-BLOCKED-NEW-WORK, STALE, ERROR, DISCONNECTED. Every read/write/stream is scoped by server-derived identity. Never inherit the existing owner Cloudflare Access session based on a cached browser state.

At all times distinguish owner private live portfolio, company public-safe aggregate, an individual broker account, internally simulated paper state, and historical backtests. A subscriber must never see company cash/P&L as their own.

## 10. Security and content rules

- No LLM in live order authorization path. No live member orders until separate approval.
- No shared owner token, owner data or one tenant's records in other members' API responses, streams, logs, exports or research submissions.
- Explicit same-origin/CSRF validation on writes; idempotency; member identity derived from verified server session; quotas checked atomically.
- Preserved account export, deletion, token revocation and billing cancellation. Preserve financial records when required, with disclosed retention.
- Accessible mobile-first layouts, keyboard operation and text equivalents for charts (WCAG 2.2 AA target); handle slow connections and stale data.
- No fabricated testimonials, fake member counts, unverified profit claims, exposure of research-only controls as live, or unsafe stock-hype rankings.
- No paid status bypassing a safety limit.

## 11. Release and success measures

A meaningful launch shows that real beta users can learn something, create a paper setup, run and interpret a bounded experiment, and understand risk. Measure return visits and helpful feedback, NOT trading volume or dollars risked. Separately measure service reliability, cost per active paper member, support time, duplicate order-prevention, cross-account denial and quota fairness.

Explicitly OUT OF SCOPE in this package: production billing, new Stripe products, real member trading, money custody/transfers, changes to founder RHEN order path, strategy auto-promotion, new options/short/leverage/crypto live scopes, public launch of user submissions, enabling unsupported RHEN modules.
