# ANEVUM — Optional community request idea (not a product pivot)

**Status: Parked feature idea, not approved for V1 navigation or launch scope.**
**Clarification received: October 12, 2026.**

## Product identity — unchanged

ANEVUM's established direction is a **public-first creative portal** inspired by Newgrounds, Envato and community software sharing. Visitors discover, try, play, browse and rate a wide range of independently created work: games, apps, AI software, tools, media, experiments and other creator submissions.

The general public is the primary audience. **Any eligible member should be able to submit their own creations**, subject to fair review, safety, and moderation. Meaningful free participation, creator discovery, engaging community activity, and a visually distinctive experience are core requirements.

We are **not** replacing the site with a requests marketplace, a commissions platform, or an app-store-only utility finder. The existing product experience and established information architecture stay intact unless the owner explicitly authorizes a separate redesign.

## Requests are one optional community use case

Members might post ideas, ask for an app to be made, suggest features, or find collaborators through normal community discussions. This needs **no dedicated top-level Requests navigation**, bounty system, commission transaction, separate workflow, or new V1 launch dependency.

If dedicated request-tracking features ever emerge, they require a new explicit product decision. Until then, treat them as ordinary community topics.

## Current work is branding only

The active decision is the official ANEVUM symbol, core colors, typography and visual design language. The owner prefers a non-A-shaped, memorable mark and a comfortable, appealing experience—without excessive cinematic or cosmic effects. Do not redesign the website layout, create new primary navigation, or change the public domain branding while this identity decision is ongoing.

Current source of truth for the existing, already-built V1 is `docs/creator-platform/LAUNCH-2027-OPERATING-PLAN.md`, the approved cinematic creator-portal direction, and its feature implementation; this note is only a correction to prevent feature ideation from being promoted to an unauthorized company pivot.
