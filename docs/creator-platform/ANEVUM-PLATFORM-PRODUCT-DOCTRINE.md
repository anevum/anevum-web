## Product definition

**ANEVUM is a general-public-first destination for discovering, trying, publishing, discussing and following independently created games, apps, AI experiences, open-source software and interactive digital work.** Internally, the mental model is “YouTube for software” — *creator channels and audiences for things people can actually try or use* — not a video hosting clone or a promise of advertising revenue.

**Working public promise:** “A world of things people make.” Supporting call to action: “Find something worth trying. Share what you make.”

The site **is ANEVUM**; community and creators are not a mandatory separate “Commons” brand. RHEN and archival V5 systems are independent historical/private products, not the public Creator V1 proposition. Preserve the existing cinematic website experience and selected **01 ORBIT** mark direction; final mark fidelity and typography remain on #335.

## Who participates

Guests explore and follow safe external project links without signing in. People with a free member account can save, follow, rate, review and earn legitimate progress. Creators submit and improve real projects. Testers, collaborators and nontechnical participants deserve recognition. Staff moderate with independently authorized permissions, not XP. Basic actions must be usable from a phone. Exact child/age eligibility is an unresolved legal and policy gate, not an “all ages” shortcut.

## What people discover

- **Games & Play:** browser games, puzzles, interactive fiction, playful experiences.
- **Apps & Utilities:** productivity, education, useful personal tools and simulations.
- **AI & Interactive:** assistants, AI-native experiences, generators and interactive creations.
- **Open Source & Developer Tools:** source repositories, libraries, components and templates.
- **Creative Experiments:** unusual prototypes and cross-disciplinary digital work.

AI involvement is a **disclosed attribute**, not a requirement or blanket genre label. Hand-built and AI-assisted creations are equally welcome.

## The project and creator experience

Every real **Project** has owner, description, category, accurate media, supported devices, real **Play / Try / Open / View source** link, distinct demo/source/docs/store URLs, external pricing/login requirements, ownership/license/AI-use disclosure, version/status/release notes, real reviews and reports. Public repo links do not imply open licensing. Clicks on external demos are *clicks*, not verified installs/plays.

Each creator has a channel-like profile with projects, releases, bio, follower relationship, authentic accomplishments and credited collaborators. Every member has a library, saved items, followed creators, contribution history and universal earned levels/achievements. Maintain staff Studio separate from creator dashboards.

**Core loop:** genuine project discovery → try/use safely → feedback/save/follow → creator improves work → legitimate XP and recognition → more valuable discovery.

### Main surfaces

- **Home:** authentic discovery and clear paths to Explore and Share, not a sales pitch or invented usage.
- **Discover:** accurate search, categories, free/browser/mobile/source/AI filters when data exists; honest editorial picks and opportunities for new creators.
- **Project:** compelling media, primary Try/Play/Open action, full project passport, creator identity, updates, reviews and report path.
- **Creator profile:** channel/portfolio, releases, follows, updates, optional moderated customization.
- **Member home/library:** saves, follows, progress, privacy, creator tools.
- **Community:** project-first feedback and discussion; wider moderated topics/challenges later.

## Integrity rules

Free participation is mandatory; no purchased XP, votes, ranking influence or compulsory subscriptions. XP is a shared, server-authoritative, auditable, reversible and anti-farming system covering testers/reviewers/creators. Exact proposed new XP values and level curve require #337 tests and approval; current staging rates are not production policy.

Real moderators and approved rights, private draft isolation, report/takedown/appeal, abuse controls, identity recovery, documented data deletion, V1 isolated restore and release rollback are gates. Never run arbitrary third-party software on ANEVUM's authenticated origin. Creator-hosted demos, repos and large binaries first; ANEVUM hosts the catalogue/community and safe bounded metadata.

## January 1, 2027 release boundary

**P0:** real project catalogue and guest discovery; secure identity/creator profiles; approved project submission/moderation; safe external Try/Play/Open actions; honest ratings/reviews, saves/follows and shared XP; accessible mobile design; real member A → staff → public guest → independent member B acceptance; operational security, restoration and owner-approved release.

**P1 conditional:** collections, creator journals, richer community, first jam, profile customization, more achievements, verified repository import and installability signals.

**P2 parked:** ANEVUM-hosted arbitrary runtime/sandboxes, creator subdomains, paid marketplace/commissions and payouts, cash-valued rewards, streaming, full chat/Discord replacement, AI coding agents and public RHEN/BYOB launch dependencies.

The active technical queue stays #325. Current production was recorded as **read-only Creator V1 foundation**; substantial account/publishing/progression capabilities have been implemented in **isolated staging** and require real authenticated acceptance. Do not mistake documentation for a deployment or move staged features into production from this decision alone.

**First acceptance slice:** one rights-cleared real project, member A drafts/submits, staff approves with reason, guest safely finds/tries it, unrelated member B reviews/saves and earns eligible progress, and recovery/account switching/moderation/reversal are verified. Evergrove / The Adventure Book is a *candidate only* pending family privacy, demo, rights, disclosures and owner release permission.
