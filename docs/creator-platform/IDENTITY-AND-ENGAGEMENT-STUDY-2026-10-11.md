# ANEVUM identity and engagement study

Session: ANEVUM.WEB.BUILD.2026-10-11.001.CREATOR-PLATFORM-SEPARATION
Date: 2026-10-11 (America/New_York)
Status: Owner feedback recorded; visual candidates and mechanics below are proposals, not approved branding or implementation.

## Latest owner feedback

The portal study is structurally "decent," but the overall look and feel, themes, marks and icons are not liked. The owner additionally wants the site to feel "addictive." Interpret this as compelling discovery, satisfying interaction, recognizable community identity and meaningful reasons to return. It does not establish approval for deceptive engagement mechanics, a particular palette, or a final logo.

Retain the current community architecture, free participation, external creator hosting and separation from V5/RHEN. Do not restart the information architecture simply to change the visual identity. Neither the earlier mint/lime marketing skin nor the charcoal/steel/amber prototype is approved brand direction.

## Useful continuity

The owner has previously described distinct collectible objects for individual levels: memorable silhouettes, sophisticated and strange artwork, not the same badge with increasingly elaborate borders. Preserve that preference in this study. Exact objects, progression thresholds, reward rules, mascots and the final visual style remain open.

## Design diagnosis and boundaries

The current screenshot uses a plain letter-A tile, conventional thin navigation icons, repeated bordered panels, a numbered hexagonal level badge and simple geometric sample artwork. Those are prototype devices, not sufficient identity assets.

Separate three asset families:
1. Brand: wordmark, primary symbol and small-size favicon variant. Assess monochrome and tiny sizes. Existing fork/stem mark is a continuity reference, not a required final mark.
2. Navigation: one coherent, readable icon family with labels and accessible controls; do not make all routine buttons ornate.
3. Collectibles: distinctive original level objects, achievement art and awards. A compact silhouette variant and rich inspectable artwork should represent the same object. Displaying a previously earned object must not misrepresent the member's actual level.

The interface should frame creator work. Avoid a large marketing hero, neon cyberpunk defaults, cheap bevels, a full fantasy-game HUD, ornamental illegibility and mandatory 3D animation. Prototype media and engagement counts must remain visibly fictional.

## Identity candidates to compare on the same portal structure

A. Artifact Arcade: premium, slightly strange digital creator venue. Warm-black surfaces, pale ceramic/silver identity, selective vermilion/cobalt accents, sculptural collectible instruments and an assertive custom wordmark. Recommended starting hypothesis, not selected branding.

B. Ink & Mischief: an illustrated creator club. Paper/ink contrast, graphic spot colors, original drawn mascot/mark, bold printed icon silhouettes and collectible illustrated objects. Expressive without generic clip-art or copied Newgrounds characters.

C. Living Machines: a grown workshop of unusual inventions. Night-green neutral surfaces, ivory, patinated metal and glass, botanical-mechanical objects, and an original seed/aperture mark. Wonder in the artwork, readable software navigation in the interface.

Comparison should include the same compact discovery screen, mobile navigation, a wordmark, icon strip, member identity and several genuinely different level-object silhouettes. These are aesthetic alternatives, not three separate products or implementation commitments.

## Reasons to return: proposed design requirements

- Discovery: a finite fresh-work selection, real followed-project updates, an optional surprise discovery and honest newcomer exposure. Do not invent daily activity when supply is thin.
- Contribution: try a project, leave a vote or useful feedback, receive a real response. Voting must confirm a server-accepted action and must not award experience repeatedly for edits.
- Identity: a recognizable profile, collection shelf, earned level object and meaningful next milestone. Progress comes from valid contribution, not idle time, autoplay or refresh spam.
- Recognition: accurate notifications when feedback helps or a creator ships a requested fix; no fabricated urgency or social proof.
- Craft: short intentional feedback for save/vote/follow actions; inspectable collectible art; optional and dismissible milestone celebrations, reduced-motion fallback and sound off by default.

No paid vote weight, purchasable XP, random paid rewards, punitive streak loss or automatic trust/moderation powers from level. Do not claim these hypotheses will cause retention before testing them with members.

## Scope of this pass

Conceptual brand exploration only. No production-domain cutover, database mutation, RHEN access, live XP system or deployed visual replacement. Next implementation should test a selected identity on the existing Discover, project and profile screens before expanding the icon and collectible library.
