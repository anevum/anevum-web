# ANEVUM / CONTINUANCE INSTITUTE
## An independent network funded by useful work
Session: ANEVUM.WEB.BUILD.2026-10-16.001.SUPPORTER-REVENUE
Status: operating model proposal; source candidate only; no live offer authorized
Prepared: 2026-10-16

### Economic purpose
ANEVUM exists to make useful and imaginative creative work accessible and help its makers find an audience. Sustainable revenue must increase the resources available to those people, not turn their attention into advertising inventory.

### Three revenue stages
1. Immediate, sell actual services: ANEVUM Studio creative portal design/implementation for real clients. This is the shortest path to a legitimate first payment because the offer can be delivered before an open community or marketplace exists. Client contracts and invoices are separate from free ANEVUM member accounts.
2. Audience-supported network: optional Founding Supporter recurring memberships ($5/month or $36/year under review), with honest cosmetic benefits after they actually ship. Open enrollment only after payments and public accounts are vetted.
3. Creator economy: finished first-party digital products, third-party creator sales, marketplace payouts and a transparent take rate (working range 5–10% subject to processor fees, taxes, and competition), implemented through Stripe Connect only after marketplace, rights, safety and payout compliance are ready. PayPal can be added if it genuinely improves conversion. No user-owned bank, promised returns, crypto speculation or proprietary money required.

### First deliverable: ANEVUM Studio / Creator Portal
Proposed retail price: $149, one-time; no subscription and no hosting upsell.
Target: independent artists, authors, game creators and small studios with real material but no clear online presentation.
What customer actually receives:
- One original responsive landing page with a deliberate visual identity, hero, clear introduction, project/features section, outbound links and contact call-to-action.
- Up to six customer-supplied projects, images or links; assets must be owned or licensed by customer.
- One revision round after presenting the first working draft.
- Source package (HTML/CSS/JS/assets) and documented deployment instructions for customer-owned static hosting; optional handoff deployment with permission and compatible existing host, not domain ownership or paid hosting.
- Clear AI-assistance disclosure when relevant; no invented testimonials or invented sales results.
Proposed timeline: five business days *after* approved brief and complete assets, subject to explicit capacity confirmation, limited to two concurrent commissions.
Not included: new hosting/domain charges, complex authentication, payment processing, custom apps/games, new original illustration packages, unlimited edits, privacy/legal policies, or ongoing maintenance.
All production delivery and price promises require owner review first; payment only after terms, scope, refund policy and customer identity are agreed.

### Order lifecycle
1. Publish one real sample of ANEVUM's own portal as a portfolio demonstration; do not invent clients.
2. Reach out personally to a small number of suitable creators with a specific improvement idea, not mass cold spam or advertising placement.
3. Scope the customer's actual deliverables, approve a written agreement with schedule, licensing and cancellation.
4. Issue a one-time Stripe invoice or Payment Link from ANEVUM's verified merchant account; no ANEVUM user sign-up required for service clients.
5. Only begin paid fulfillment after Stripe confirms payment. Record income, processor fees, refunds and fulfillment work in simple bookkeeping.
6. Deliver with an acceptance checklist, backup of editable source, transfer instructions and one revision window.
7. Ask satisfied customers whether they voluntarily want their work credited as examples, and obtain permission before publishing.

### Recurring membership
Proposed $5/month or $36/year. Benefits must be currently deliverable; proposed orbit insignia has an explicit visibility opt-in (default OFF) and is never XP or an earned medal. No paid boost in feeds or search. Free accounts keep core participation, posts, discovery, creation and earned rewards.
Not a donation/charity claim; it is a commercial membership. No revenue share or guaranteed investment return to supporters.

### Independence principles
Never monetize visitor surveillance, unwanted feed advertising, private message access, purchased achievements, pay-for-reach feeds, pay-to-win mechanics or creator lock-in. The eventual creator marketplace should make its commissions, payout timing and refund responsibility obvious before a buyer pays.

### Decision gates
Owner: approve the exact service offer, price, available hours, seller legal identity, customer refund/cancellation policy, tax/bookkeeping setup and contact method.
Engineering: pass test Stripe lifecycle, D1 migration and rollback, webhook settlement/out-of-order/refund tests, account deletion treatment, stable signup, privacy opt-in and accessible mobile UI.
Release: owner explicitly authorizes staging deployment separately and live Stripe launch separately. Never merge to apex countdown or enable public signup by accident.

### First realistic milestone
**One legitimately paid, scoped and fulfilled creator project.** This proves someone will pay ANEVUM for useful creative work without compromising community values. Next: replicate the delivery once, document what cost/time was involved, then decide which reusable pieces can be sold as first-party digital assets.
