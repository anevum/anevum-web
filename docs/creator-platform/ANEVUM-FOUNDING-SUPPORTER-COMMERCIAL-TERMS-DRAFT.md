**NOT PUBLISHED — NOT LEGAL ADVICE — NOT AN ACTIVE OFFER**

## Offer summary
ANEVUM Founding Supporter is an optional, commercial membership to support operation of an independent creative community. Normal ANEVUM accounts and earned recognition remain free.

Proposed US-dollar recurring payment choices:
- $5.00 per month, billed monthly.
- $36.00 per year, billed yearly (equivalent to $3/month, but charged annually).
- Applicable taxes, if any, must be calculated and disclosed before payment. The current inactive Stripe Price objects have unspecified tax behavior; the seller must choose a lawful tax approach before activation.
- Same benefits for monthly and annual plans. Paying a year upfront does not instantly unlock age-based marks.

## What supporters actually receive once live and functioning
A verified paid subscription, when active and not under billing review, unlocks cosmetic recognition:
- A founder insignia, privately visible by default. The member can opt into showing it on a public profile and in public community cards/activity.
- Four collectible seals for subscription ages 0/90/180/365 completed days.
- Five portrait frame selections and five animated banner atmospheres with reduced-motion accommodations, according to the documented unlock schedule.
- Ability to change cosmetic selection from the member Supporter Studio and manage billing from Stripe's Customer Portal.

Cosmetics are limited to currently active memberships. The member may opt not to display public recognition without losing subscription benefits. A new subscription starts a new reward tenure; do not promise grandfathered tenure. An interrupted subscription must be accurately described on the product page.

This does **not** buy XP, level icons, earned medals, moderation powers, creator fees, public reach, discovery ranking, priority support, access to protected member information, financial instruments or investment returns.

## Cancellation and renewal
Recurring subscriptions renew until canceled. ANEVUM will provide an accessible account-linked Stripe Customer Portal for changing payment information and requesting cancellation, subject to Stripe settings. Under the proposed cancellation policy, a cancellation requested before the next billing date should prevent subsequent renewals, with supporter cosmetics remaining available through the already paid period unless the account is subject to a refund, dispute or other lawful limitation.

**Before launch, confirm the exact live Customer Portal cancellation-at-period-end settings** and test cancellation before renewal and cancellation followed by resubscription. Do not publish misleading claims that a pending cancellation can be executed if it cannot.

## Refunds, disputes, customer assistance
A fair and legally reviewed refund standard must be chosen and implemented before sale. Do not claim a blanket no-refund policy where local law provides cancellation/refund rights. Customer email/support channel must be verified end-to-end. When a refund or charge dispute is detected, server-side decorative entitlements pause until reconciliation; actual customer remedy/merchant case review must not be silently automated from the user's cosmetic settings.

Account deletion needs a safe and tested way to cancel or explain active subscriptions and retain only legally required accounting data. Deleting an ANEVUM account must not leave a hidden recurring charge.

## Privacy
Payments use Stripe-hosted checkout. ANEVUM's backend stores a canonical member-to-Stripe customer association, subscribed Price ID, subscription status/period, event identifiers, created-time information, and the member's optional display preferences. No raw card number is needed in the ANEVUM app. Only an explicit opt-in supporter badge and cosmetic appearance may appear on public member surfaces. Never publish a customer's email, Stripe IDs, billing disputes, charge dates, invoice identifiers or financial history as part of supporter recognition.

## Changes to benefits
Perks can be improved over time, but the seller should communicate any material removal or price change before it affects a member's renewal. No benefits that depend on unbuilt future features are guaranteed by this offer.
