### What we sell
**Creator Portal** — a distinctive single-page website for an independent maker, game developer, musician, illustrator or small studio. It explains who they are, shows their real work, and directs visitors to a verified way to contact or follow them.

**Proposed pilot fee: US$149 once.** No ongoing ANEVUM subscription, ad placement, percentage of future revenue or domain/hosting upsell.

Proposed fixed scope:
- One original, responsive HTML/CSS website with accessible site navigation, creator introduction, and a "selected work" section for up to six customer-owned projects.
- Supplied project screenshots/artwork, titles, captions and real external project/social links.
- Verified customer contact call-to-action, at least one usable contact or public profile destination.
- One coherent typographic, color and visual treatment. Subtle motion only when useful, with reduced-motion support.
- One reasonable revision round based on a single consolidated feedback message.
- Transferable, editable source code package and deployment instructions for an existing customer-owned static host.
- Honest AI-assistance, media licensing, and third-party code disclosure.

Excluded unless separately quoted: hosting fees, domain registration, mailboxes, backends, sign-in, ecommerce, checkout, custom games/apps, moderation, SEO ranking promises, copywriting for more than supplied basics, ongoing maintenance or an unlimited revision loop.

Target delivery for each accepted project: **five business days after receiving a complete brief and rights-cleared assets**. Proposed capacity: two projects simultaneously. Confirm founder availability before advertising an actual delivery date.

### Demonstration
The files demo/index.html and demo/styles.css are a **working offline sample**, not a client project or claim of previous work. It intentionally uses fictional placeholder entries, prominently marks itself DEMO, has no third-party assets or tracking, and does not collect customer information. Client delivery must replace every sample project and placeholder contact. The visual motif is an original general orbital illustration, not the proprietary ANEVUM brand mark.

To preview locally: open demo/index.html in a browser. A simple static web server is also sufficient; no npm install, Node backend or paid hosting required.

### How a pilot customer order will work (after approval)
1. Customer submits a brief privately through a real, verified communication route that ANEVUM has tested. Do not solicit sensitive identity/payment information in a public GitHub issue.
2. ANEVUM confirms scope, rights and asset availability. If the project needs bespoke app/backend development, quote separately rather than claiming it fits this price.
3. Both parties approve a written order summary: scope, number of projects, delivery target, revision window, cancellation/refund rules, hosting duties, licensing, communication method and fees.
4. ANEVUM creates a one-time USD Stripe invoice/Payment Link specifically for that accepted scope. **Never take an unexplained payment before agreeing on deliverables.**
5. Upon Stripe-confirmed payment, work begins. Record revenue, processing fees, fulfillment effort and applicable tax accounting. Do not equate gross sales with personal take-home income.
6. Deliver a live review preview under customer-approved storage or an offline zipped static build. **Do not expose private assets to a public ANEVUM repo without permission.**
7. Apply one consolidated revision round, validate final links and hand over full source plus simple deployment instructions.
8. Get explicit written permission before displaying the finished site as an ANEVUM case study or disclosing the customer relationship.
