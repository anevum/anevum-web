# Public Site Snapshot — Transcosmic / Publishing Era

This document preserves the public-facing concepts that were present in ANEVUM's publisher-first website before the 2026-09-25 archive transition.

## ANEVUM

ANEVUM was presented as an independent publisher centered on finished books and reader tools. REPLY was the active publication priority, with the Wiki, Lattice, RHENLINK, and Store positioned as supporting systems around the books.

## REPLY

REPLY was presented as The Transcosmic / Book One and the intended public entry point into the setting.

The public premise described a world like ours discovering that it is not alone. Contact with Veyra introduced extraordinary medicine, energy, manufacturing, AI, and the possibility of a Skygate, while raising questions about scarcity, identity, family, aging, industry, and what being human still means.

The book was publicly described as a speculative novel mixing mystery, horror, romance, and social change. Its status was revision / release preparation.

## The Transcosmic

The Transcosmic was described as a shared fictional universe revealed through finished books rather than front-loaded lore.

Public reference systems were intended to organize material that readers had already encountered in published work.

## Wiki

The earlier public Wiki was intended as a searchable reference for released people, places, institutions, technologies, events, and concepts. Development canon was intended to remain private until publication made it appropriate to expose.

## Lattice

Lattice was intended as a relational exploration layer connecting released records without replacing the books with lore documentation.

## Store and reader tools

The Store was intended to list real, verified books and related objects only when they could actually be ordered. RHENLINK was intended to hold reader identity, release-update preferences, and later saves, purchases, achievements, and reading progress.

## Archive status

All of the above is historical context. It is preserved here so the creative work remains recoverable while active ANEVUM documentation stays focused on the current personal-project system.
